\begin{keybox}[How to use this document]
This is the final, complete record of the project. Part~\ref{sec:onepage} (the
project on one page, the numbers cheat sheet, the grading map) answers most
questions. The next parts explain every component of the \pg fuzzer in depth:
what it does, why it is built this way, and what it was measured to do. Then come
the results of the final campaigns (Section~\ref{part:results}), the paper and the
plan we followed, every bug we reported with its upstream state
(Part~\ref{part:bugs}), the generalized fuzzer for DuckDB, SQLite, Turso and
DataFusion with its three filed fixes (Part~\ref{part:generalized}), who did
what (Part~\ref{part:team}), the deliverables and how to reproduce every result
(Part~\ref{part:deliverables}), and a long list of questions with short answers.
Every number comes from a run recorded in the repository (path given).
\end{keybox}
