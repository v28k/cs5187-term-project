\section*{Statement of Contributions}
\label{sec:contrib}
%% Course rule: each member justifies their contribution in a signed statement,
%% backed by the commit history (GitHub users: v28k = Venu Kummari,
%% Blazing-bhuvi = Bhuvan Chilukuti, doma2542 = Gowtham Reddy).
Every member can explain every part of the project; the split below records who
did most of the work on each part. The commit history of the public repository
shows the same split (GitHub users \texttt{v28k}, \texttt{Blazing-bhuvi} and
\texttt{doma2542}).

\paragraph{Venu Kummari} Design and implementation of \tool: the typed grammar
and its generator, the metamorphic oracles on derivation trees, the plan-variant,
cross-version, row-level-security and cardinality oracles, the executor and hang
oracle, triage with tree and token reduction, the LLM gate, the re-injected bug
benchmark and the evaluation harness. Reported BUG \#19768 (\texttt{JSON\_TABLE})
and BUG \#19793 (\texttt{range @> NULL}) with patches, and BUG \#19826
(\texttt{corr()} under partial aggregation, fixed upstream with his patch). Wrote the paper. Built
the generalized fuzzer for DuckDB, SQLite, Turso and DataFusion
(Section~\ref{sec:beyond}) and wrote its five upstream fixes.

\paragraph{Bhuvan Chilukuti} The \rqref{3} campaign on the re-injected bug
benchmark and its attribution. Cross-version, metamorphic and audit-driven bug
hunts on recent \pg features. Reported, with patches, the \texttt{NOT IN}
anti-join bug, the \texttt{IGNORE NULLS} hang, the duplicate statistics
expression assertion and the non-finite MCV frequencies; answered the
maintainers on these threads.

\paragraph{Gowtham Reddy} Server builds, the \rqref{1}, \rqref{2} and \rqref{4}
campaigns (35 trials) with their reports, the LLM arm with Gemini Flash over ten
commits, and the triage of the campaign candidates. Reported BUG \#19802
(\texttt{MERGE}) and BUG \#19803 (parallel \texttt{Append}), both fixed upstream
(reporter and tester), and reported BUG \#19771 (\texttt{to\_char}) and wrote its
fix, committed upstream.
