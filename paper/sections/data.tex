\section*{Data Availability}
\label{sec:data}
The replication package is the public course repository
\url{https://github.com/PASS-IITG/cs5187-term-project-blazing-bhuvi} (MIT
license). The generalized fuzzer of Section~\ref{sec:beyond} is on branch
\texttt{generalized} of \url{https://github.com/v28k/cs5187-term-project}. Its \texttt{README.md} gives exact dependencies, a
one-command reproduction of the headline result, and every upstream report with
its state. It contains
the source of \tool with its unit tests, the grammar and the schema. It also
contains the benchmark: the 23 fix commits, their PoCs and the scripts that
build the fixed, solo and injected servers. Finally, it holds the experiment
configurations, the run logs and reports of every trial, the triage verdicts,
and the LLM ledger with every prompt and response, so that \rqref{4} can be
replayed without calling the model.

\section*{Use of Generative AI}
\tool calls a large language
model as part of its method (Section~\ref{sec:llm}). In the evaluation this was
Google Gemini Flash (Section~\ref{sec:eval}); every prompt and response, with
the model that served it, is in the replication package. An AI coding assistant
helped with parts of the implementation and with drafting text. The authors
designed the approach, reviewed all code and text, and checked every statement,
citation and number. All numbers reported here were produced by running the
tool and scripts.
