\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\tool generates SQL as typed derivation trees, rewrites the trees into
metamorphic checks, compares each query with its plan variants and the last
release, and extends its grammar from recent commits behind a server-side
gate. We reported nine \pg bugs found with it, six of them fixed upstream (five with
our patches) and one more confirmed. Its grammar reaches 36.7\% of backend lines against 22.2\% for random
mutation, yet the precision of the oracles, not the reach of the generator,
decided how useful its findings were. With a dialect overlay and a small adapter per engine, the same core
tests DuckDB, SQLite, Turso and DataFusion and found three more bugs there, whose
fixes we filed upstream (Section~\ref{sec:beyond}).
